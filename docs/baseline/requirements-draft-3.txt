1. Executive Summary

Spellzee is evolving from multiple operational tools and manual workflows into a controlled, connected ERP. The immediate
build is a Student Operations & Delivery Platform, but the product should be architected as the foundation of a broader Spellzee
ERP.
The purpose is not to reproduce spreadsheets or simply combine screens. The ERP should become a system of record and a
system of control: it should know who the student is, what they purchased, what was scheduled, what was delivered, what was
taught, who owns the relationship, what problems occurred, what capacity is available, what changes were approved, and what
should happen next.
The foundation should be built before advanced AI. Reliable identities, relationships, workflows, history, permissions and event
data will create the dataset required for later forecasting, recommendations and automation.

2. Why Spellzee Needs an ERP

· Multiple tools currently hold different parts of the student, sales, calling, class and communication journey.
· Allocation uses operational sheets and separate LMS/classroom processes.
· Parents may communicate through multiple WhatsApp accounts, making ownership and history difficult to control.
· Student duplicates can arise when returning families use a different email/phone or slightly different spelling.
· Teacher availability is complex and cannot be represented adequately by a simple Monday­Friday grid.
· Compensation and rescheduling require policy-based decisions to protect both student entitlement and business capacity.
· Teacher training, observation, performance and incentives are currently substantial processes that need structured records.
· Management needs to know capacity versus demand and future recruitment needs, not merely today's availability.
· Important changes need approval, segregation of duties and auditability.
· Student history must remain intact when teachers, schedules, courses or coordinators change.

3. Product Vision & Principles

3.1 Vision

Build Spellzee ERP as the trusted operating system for the complete student, employee and delivery lifecycle.

3.2 Core Principles

· One permanent Spellzee identity per student/person.
· Student, enrollment, subscription, payment, schedule and session are distinct concepts linked by identifiers.
· Never silently overwrite important history.
· Do not permanently delete critical business records without exceptional authorization.
· Every critical action is attributable and auditable.
· Maker and checker are separated for sensitive actions.
· Business rules should be configurable.
· Capacity must be measurable and forecastable.
· Communication, tickets, academic records and financial records are connected but not conflated.
· Spellzee owns its core identifiers even when external systems are integrated.
· Foundation first; analytics and AI after data quality is established.
North-star principle: Nothing important should happen invisibly.

13. Capacity Planning & Workforce Forecasting

Availability answers whether a person is free at a point in time. Capacity planning answers how much Spellzee can
realistically deliver and how much additional demand it can absorb.

13.1 Capacity Dimensions

· Teacher total capacity.

Spellzee ERP -- Master Product & Business Requirements -- Draft 3  Page 7
· Occupied/allocated capacity.
· Free capacity.
· Capacity by day/time slot.
· Subject/level capacity.
· Language/bilingual capacity such as Tamil-English or Hindi-English.
· 1-to-1 versus group capacity.
· Teacher leave/unavailability.
· Temporary capacity from student breaks.
· Capacity released by expected subscription completion.
· Teacher utilization.

13.2 Current vs Forecast Capacity

Current capacity is available now. Forecast capacity includes known or predicted future releases. A student taking a two-month
break can temporarily release teacher capacity; the system should represent the expected release period and avoid treating it as
permanent capacity.

13.3 Capacity vs Demand

Compare current and forecast capacity against expected new admissions. Example: 32 free sessions + 8 expected releases
versus 45 expected demand = projected gap of 5. Management can then decide on recruitment, reallocation or demand
management.

13.4 Recruitment Forecast

Capacity gaps by subject, language, level and time should feed HR recruitment planning. Historical admissions, breaks, churn,
teacher attrition and seasonality can later support prediction.

13.5 Utilization

Measure allocated/used capacity versus available capacity. Avoid optimizing for 100% utilization; management should define a
healthy target range that preserves flexibility.

15.2 Reminder Rules

For a 3 PM class, the system can send a configurable reminder several hours before the class, and optionally another reminder
closer to start time. The exact timing is a business policy setting.

15.3 Cancellation Rules

Spellzee should define a configurable cancellation cutoff. If the parent provides sufficient advance notice, the session credit may
remain protected. A late cancellation may consume credit or follow a defined exception policy. This must be transparent
internally and consistently applied.

15.4 Teacher/Spellzee Failure

Teacher absence or verified teacher/Spellzee-side failure should generally protect the student's entitlement and trigger
compensation. Student-side technical failure may follow a separate policy.

15.5 Compensation

Compensation is a separate session and must not modify the normal recurring schedule. A Monday missed session can produce
a Saturday compensation class while Monday remains the regular schedule.

16. Completion & Payment Forecasting

When a student starts a 12-, 20- or other session plan, the system can calculate a planned completion date from start date,
schedule and session entitlement. Actual attendance and compensation then produce a projected completion date.
· Planned completion date.
· Projected completion date.
· Delay versus original plan.
· Missed sessions and reasons.
· Compensation backlog.
· Expected renewal/payment window for internal planning.
· Forecast changes as sessions are completed or missed.
This forecast should help Operations and Finance understand when the next payment/renewal may occur without exposing
internal business calculations unnecessarily to parents.

        22. Governance, Segregation of Duties & Approvals

        22.1 Why

        ERP controls should reduce accidental corruption, unauthorized changes, duplicate identities and financial manipulation. The
        system should support management control without making normal work unnecessarily difficult.

        22.2 Role-Based Access

        Permissions should determine who may view, create, edit, approve, cancel, merge, archive, export or administer each class of
        information.

        22.3 Maker­Checker

        For sensitive actions, the requester should not approve their own request. Examples: refunds, duplicate merges, historical
        corrections, high-impact subscription changes and restricted deletion.

23. Integrations

23.1 Merithub

Merithub is the live classroom/LMS specialist. Spellzee should create/update classes through the integration, store external IDs
and receive relevant attendance, recording and status events. The integration should include authentication, error handling,
retry, logging and webhook validation.

23.2 TeliCRM / Delicio

During transition, external sales/student records can be mapped to Spellzee IDs. The long-term source of truth and migration
boundary must be explicitly decided.

23.3 FreeJump

Where API support permits, capture call time, duration, coordinator, purpose/outcome and attempts for onboarding/coordinator
calls.

23.4 WhatsApp

Select an approved provider/API for controlled parent communication, templates, delivery status and conversation mapping.
Exact provider remains a discovery decision.

23.5 Integration Principle

External IDs map to Spellzee IDs. External systems should not silently overwrite critical Spellzee records.

32. Product North Star

Spellzee ERP should not become a larger spreadsheet.

It should become a controlled operating system for Spellzee: one identity, one connected history, clear ownership,
capacity-backed commitments, policy-driven delivery, protected student entitlement, accountable employees, governed financial
actions, measurable academic outcomes and a reliable foundation for future intelligence.

The document should now be treated as the comprehensive product/business baseline. The next discipline is not to jump
directly into code. The next step is to validate the workflows and decisions, then translate this baseline into a detailed product
requirements specification, UX flows, technical architecture, data model, integration contracts, permission matrix and
implementation backlog.
